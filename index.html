About

Brick Blair is a Brooklyn singer-songwriter. A baritone with indie folk and alt-country roots, writing songs he couldn't get out of his head, some of them for fifteen years.

Almost all his gear comes from charity shops and estate sales. He rebuilds it, then writes on it.
